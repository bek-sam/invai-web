# See your true profit and record ad spend

**What you see:** Revenue numbers from the marketplace that don't match what actually lands in your
bank account, or a design that "sells a lot" but you're not sure if it's worth the effort.

**Why:** **Analytics → Profit** takes revenue and subtracts channel fees, blanks, transfers, shipping
labels, packaging, labor and ad spend, so the number you see is what's actually left — "estimate" is
shown next to any order whose fee data isn't final yet.

## See true profit
1. Go to **Analytics → Profit**. Pick a **Period**.
2. Switch the view **By order**, **By design**, **By blank**, **By channel** or **By day**. Rows open
   that period's order list.
3. Click a row for the **Fee breakdown** or **Order profit** — every cost line, with estimates
   marked.
4. Click **Export CSV** to take the numbers elsewhere, or **Recompute** if a number looks stale after
   you changed a cost.

## Record a refund
1. On an order's profit breakdown, click **Record refund**, enter the **Refund amount** and
   **Refund date**.
2. A refund entered wrong can be undone: click **Void**, and say **Why void this refund?**.

## Record ad spend
1. Go to **Analytics → Ad spend**, click **Add spend** (one entry: date, channel, amount, campaign)
   or **Import CSV** for many at once — click **Download template** first for the exact columns.
2. Ad spend feeds the "Ads" cost line in true profit, so recording it moves your net profit number
   down to what it really is.

![Profit page split by design with the Export CSV button](../img/profit-and-ad-spend/01-profit-by-design.png)

## Check it worked
The **Net profit** and **Margin** numbers update (a toast says "profit is being recomputed" right
after a refund or ad spend change — give it a few seconds and refresh).

## Still stuck?
- Stop and contact support if net profit goes negative for a design that's clearly making money, or a
  cost line (like Channel fees or Blanks) is at $0 for every order on a channel you know charges fees.
- Send: the period, the design or order number, and a screenshot of the fee breakdown. Don't send the
  buyer's address.
