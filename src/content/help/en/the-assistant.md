# Ask the assistant about your shop

**What you see:** A question you'd normally have to dig through several screens to answer — "which
blanks will run out this week", "am I shipping on time", "is this design trending".

**Why:** **Assistant** answers using only your shop's own orders, costs, stock and market data — it's
read-only and buyer names or addresses never leave InvAI. It's not a general chatbot; every answer is
built from real numbers in your account.

## Fix it
1. Go to **Assistant**, click **New chat** if you want to start over.
2. Type your question in the box (placeholder: "Ask about profit, orders, stock…"), or click a
   suggested question like "Which blanks will run out in the next 7 days?" or "How many orders are at
   risk of shipping late?".
3. Click **Send**. While it's working you'll see notes like "Checked orders" or "Checked shipping" —
   that's the assistant looking up your real data before answering.
4. Ask a follow-up in the same chat — it remembers the conversation.

![Assistant chat with a suggested question and a checked-data note](../img/the-assistant/01-assistant-chat.png)

## Check it worked
The answer names real numbers from your shop (an order count, a dollar figure, a design name) — not a
generic answer. Your remaining **AI credits** show under the chat box.

## Still stuck?
- Stop and contact support if the assistant gives a number that doesn't match what you see on
  **Profit** or **Shipping** for the same period, or it says it can't check something that's clearly
  in your account.
- Send: the exact question you asked and the answer. Don't send the buyer's address — the assistant
  shouldn't have shown you one in the first place.
