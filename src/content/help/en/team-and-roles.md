# Invite a teammate and set their role or floor PIN

**What you see:** A new hire who needs to sign in to InvAI, or floor staff who only ever use a tablet
and don't need an email account at all.

**Why:** Roles decide what a person can see and do (Owner, Admin, Office, Designer, Presser, Packer,
Receiver, Vendor). Office staff sign in with email; floor staff (pressers, packers, receivers) usually
sign in on a station tablet with a personal PIN instead.

## Invite someone with email sign-in
1. Go to **Settings → Team**, click **Invite**.
2. Enter their **Name**, email and **Role**. Click **Send invite**. They get an email link that lasts
   a week.
3. Invite still pending? Click **Resend** to send it again with a fresh link, or **Revoke** to cancel
   it.

## Add floor-only staff (no email)
1. Click **Invite**, turn on **No email: floor PIN only**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Role** (Presser, Packer or Receiver), then set a **Floor PIN** (4–6
   digits, unique in your company — it's shown only once). Click **Add and set PIN**.
3. They sign in on any paired station tablet with that PIN — see [Floor tablet
   setup](floor-tablet-setup.md).

## Change a role, reset a PIN, or remove someone
1. Find them on **Settings → Team**, click their **Role** to change it — this applies right away.
2. Click **PIN** to set a new floor PIN for them.
3. Click **Deactivate** to sign them out everywhere and turn off their PIN; **Reactivate** brings
   them back. A company always needs at least one Owner, so InvAI won't let you deactivate the last
   one.

![Team page with Invite button and a role dropdown](../img/team-and-roles/01-invite-team.png)

## Check it worked
The person's row shows their new role, or **PIN set**, or **Deactivated**. They should be able to
sign in (or be blocked, if deactivated) right away.

## Still stuck?
- Stop and contact support if a PIN "taken" message won't let you set any PIN for someone, or an
  invited person's email link says it doesn't work and they haven't used it yet.
- Send: the person's name, their role, and the exact message shown. Don't send their PIN.
