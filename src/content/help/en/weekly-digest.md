# Get (or stop getting) the weekly business review by email

**What you see:** A "Your week in review is ready" card on **Today** with a prompt "Get this in your
inbox every Monday?", or an email you want to stop receiving.

**Why:** The weekly business review is a summary — revenue, net profit, margin, orders, on-time rate,
and this week's actions — built every Monday from your shop's own numbers. It's opt-in by email; it
always shows up in-app on the **Digests** page either way.

## Opt in
1. From the **Today** card, click **Email me every week**. Or go to **Settings → Notifications**,
   turn on **Weekly review**, and pick the **Day** and **Time** it should send.
2. Want to see one before Monday? Click **Send me a preview now**.

## Read a past week
1. Go to **Digests** in the left menu. Click any **Week of {{day}}** to open it.
2. Each action (like "Ship 3 overdue orders" or "Reorder {{blank}}") can be marked helpful or not —
   your feedback shapes future weeks.

## Unsubscribe
1. Click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of the weekly email — no sign-in needed. Confirm **Stop
   getting the weekly business review by email?**.
2. Changed your mind right after? The confirmation page offers **Undo** for a short time. After that,
   turn it back on from **Settings → Notifications**.

![Weekly review opt-in card on the Today page](../img/weekly-digest/01-digest-optin.png)

## Check it worked
After opting in, **Settings → Notifications** shows "Getting it by email" for your account. After
unsubscribing, it shows "Not by email", and the page reads "You won't get the weekly review by email
any more."

## Still stuck?
- Stop and contact support if you unsubscribed but keep getting the email, or a teammate who should
  get it says they never have.
- Send: the account email and roughly when you clicked unsubscribe or opted in.
