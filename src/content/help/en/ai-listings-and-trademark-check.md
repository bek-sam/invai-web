# Draft a listing with AI and clear a trademark risk flag

**What you see:** A blank listing to write for a new design, or a draft that says "Trademark risk
NN/100" and won't let you approve, publish or export it.

**Why:** **Listings → AI Drafts** writes a title, tags, description and bullet points per channel and
checks them against that channel's rules and a database of registered clothing marks before a person
ever signs off — so a listing can't accidentally use a trademarked term and get the shop an IP strike.

## Draft a listing
1. Go to **Listings → AI Drafts**, click **Draft listings**.
2. Choose a design and the **Channels** to draft for. Add a **Brief** if you want (e.g. "funny, for
   dog moms, desert vibes"). Click **Generate**.
3. Review the draft: **Title**, **Bullet points**, **Description**, **Tags**. Not happy? Click
   **Regenerate** and say what should change (e.g. "shorter title, more gift-focused"), or edit the
   text yourself and click save.
4. Click **Approve**, then **Publish** (or **Export CSV** for a channel whose API isn't approved yet
   — copy it in by hand).

## Clear a trademark risk flag
1. High risk ("this listing cannot be approved, published or exported until the flagged text is
   changed"): open the draft, find the **Matched text**, and edit the title, tags, description or
   bullets so it no longer matches a registered mark. There is no override for high risk.
2. Medium risk ("a recorded compliance review is required"): click **Record review**, explain in at
   least a few words **why this is acceptable to publish**, and save. The listing can then be
   approved, published or exported.
3. To check a phrase before drafting, go to **Listings → Trademark check**, type it into **Text to check**,
   click **Check risk**.

![Trademark risk banner on a listing draft, with Record review](../img/ai-listings-and-trademark-check/01-trademark-risk.png)

## Check it worked
The banner changes to "reviewed by compliance, cleared to publish" (medium) or the risk score drops
(high, after the text changes), and **Approve**/**Publish**/**Export CSV** become available again.

## Still stuck?
- Stop and contact support if a listing you're sure is safe keeps flagging as high risk, or one you
  suspect is risky passes with no flag at all.
- Send: the design name, the channel, and the **Matched text** shown on the flag. Don't send the
  buyer's address (listings don't have one, but check before sending a screenshot).
