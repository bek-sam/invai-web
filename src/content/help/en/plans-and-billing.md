# Choose a plan, buy AI credits, or fix a payment problem

**What you see:** A banner about your free trial ending, a "Your last payment didn't go through"
message, or you just want to see what your plan includes.

**Why:** Your plan sets how many orders a month, users and channel connections you get, plus a
monthly allowance of AI credits (used by listing drafts, the assistant and trademark checks). Only
the account **Owner** can change plans or buy credits.

## Choose or change a plan
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**. Under **Plans**, click **Choose {{plan}}** for the plan you want.
2. Moving to a smaller plan: InvAI shows what that plan includes and warns that imports, invites or
   new connections stop if you're using more than the new plan allows. Click **Switch plan** to
   confirm.
3. Don't need a paid plan right now? Click **Switch to the free plan**, confirm **Switch to free**.

## Buy more AI credits
1. On the same page, find **AI credits**, click **Buy {{n}} credits** for a one-time pack — credits
   are added as soon as payment goes through.
2. See where credits went under **AI credit history**: every draft, assistant answer and trademark
   check that spent one.

## Fix a payment problem
1. A "Your last payment didn't go through" banner means your card needs updating. Click **Manage
   billing** (or **Update payment** on the banner) to fix it.
2. If checkout won't open, wait a minute and try again — a "Couldn't open checkout" message usually
   clears up on retry.

![Billing page showing the current plan and AI credit balance](../img/plans-and-billing/01-billing-plan.png)

## Check it worked
**Current plan** on **Settings → Billing** shows the new plan name, and the **AI credits** count goes
up right after a purchase. A cleared payment problem removes the past-due banner.

## Still stuck?
- Stop and contact support if you were charged but your plan or credits didn't update after a few
  minutes, or **Manage billing** won't open at all.
- Send: the plan you chose (or the pack size), and roughly when you paid. Don't send your card
  number — InvAI never asks for it directly.
